# Florida landing page design

## Overview

Extension of the existing King Iron Works identity, not a rebrand. The October 21 second owner direction is current and supersedes the earlier monochrome controls, black logo and light split hero. Use the official white-and-gold logo, charcoal masthead, photographic hero and project-led content order.

## Colors

Charcoal #15191c, white #ffffff, subdued text #555555, pale stone #f2f0eb and warm portfolio background #f5f3ef. Primary controls use brass #c59a4b with #141719 text. The official SVG retains its white and gold artwork (#FFFFFF, #C38D35, #D4A034).

## Typography

Space Grotesk headings, Inter body. Hero display uses clamp(48px,5.6vw,80px), weight 600 and line-height 1.05; at 1100px and below it is 64px, and at 760px and below 44px with line-height 1.07. General section headings use clamp(32px,3.5vw,50px); portfolio headings use clamp(36px,4vw,58px), becoming 36px on phone. Body is 16px/1.65; hero supporting text is 19px desktop and 16px phone.

## Layout

Content width is capped at 1248px with 48px desktop gutters; below 1000px, gutters are 24px. Section spacing uses clamp(64px,8vw,112px). Header height is 114px desktop and 92px phone, excluding the utility strip.

Page order: company masthead; full-width photographic hero; four-service jump navigation; staircase portfolio and structural bridge feature; detailed service rows; process; Florida service area; FAQ; inline estimate form; company footer. The bridge spans the content container, with its full 4:3 image contained in the frame. Hero photography uses a dark directional overlay behind text.

## Elevation & Depth

No card shadows. Photography, tonal sections and divider lines supply depth. The fixed mobile contact bar uses a subtle upward shadow. Controls transition transform and opacity over 160ms; reduced-motion disables transitions and animation.

## Shapes

Sharp photographic frames. Primary buttons have 2px corners; form fields retain 4px corners.

## Components

Official white-and-gold SVG logo: intrinsic ratio approximately 1.73:1, displayed within a 154×90px desktop box and 128×74px phone box using object-fit contain. Desktop navigation links to Home, Services, Projects, Our company, Locations and Contact. At 760px and below, a native disclosure labeled Menu exposes company navigation and the Florida estimate link below the masthead.

Primary actions are gold. Focus outlines use 2px currentColor on links/buttons and charcoal on fields. Mobile controls are at least 44px, inputs use 16px type, and the bottom contact bar accounts for the safe area and hides while entering form data. Service planning details and FAQs use native disclosures.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve official logo artwork, real project photographs and location-neutral portfolio attribution.
- Do keep projects ahead of detailed service copy and retain the structural bridge feature.
- Don't imply portfolio photographs were made in Florida.
- Don't invent reviews, scale metrics, licensing, credentials or guarantees.
